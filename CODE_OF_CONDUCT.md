# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Accepting feedback gracefully
- Focusing on community safety and maintainability

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, doxxing, or abusive conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit contributions that violate this code of conduct and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or severe misconduct.

## Reporting

Report issues privately through the security and governance channels described in `SECURITY.md` and `docs/GOVERNANCE.md`.
